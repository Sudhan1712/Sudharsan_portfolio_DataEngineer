About

Pipelines that move data from raw files to reports.

I am a data engineer with about 2.8 years of experience building and optimizing scalable batch data pipelines on AWS.

My work is Python, PySpark and SQL for data processing, transformation and automation. I develop ETL workflows with AWS Glue, Lambda and Step Functions, and design S3-based data lakes with Redshift and Athena for analytics.

I am comfortable with data modeling using star schemas, data quality validation, and pipeline monitoring. I have delivered data solutions in the e-commerce and insurance domains.
